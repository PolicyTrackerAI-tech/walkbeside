# GPL Collection Worklist — Wasatch Front first, Sacramento second

_Compiled 2026-08-18 from two web-research passes (~140 tool calls). This is the
operating document for the §14 "parallel data lane" in `docs/BUSINESS_PLAN.md`:
the pilot-blocking sprint to get real funeral-home price data and a vetted
directory before the first pilot family activates (~Oct 1)._

## The rules (law for this lane)

1. **No pretexting, ever.** All collection is open, FCA-survey style: we
   identify ourselves when we request anything. Posing as a bereaved family is
   the one collection method that could poison the brand.
2. **Key every record to the FD/establishment license number printed on the
   GPL**, not to the URL or the building. One PDF can cover multiple licensed
   locations (Dignity, CFCS, Goff, Memorial), and one building can house three
   licensees (1001 11th Ave SLC).
3. **Rank by the effective date printed inside the document**, never by search
   rank. Stale copies outnumber current ones in the index (City View's posted
   GPL is dated 2019; East Lawn has four vintages live).
4. **Record provenance per GPL**: `posted` (harvested from the home's own
   site), `fca_hosted` (FCA survey copy, note survey date), `requested`
   (sent to us on open request), `family_consented` (uploaded via the product).
5. Never trust third-party directory prices (Parting cites a 2015 GPL for a
   home whose live one is 2026).
6. A 301 from an independent domain to dignitymemorial.com is an
   ownership-change signal — capture it.

## Wasatch Front gotchas

1. **Brands, not buildings:** 82 locations ≈ 45 unique GPLs. Memorial (8 homes,
   one GPL), Lindquist (7), Larkin (4), Goff (3), Berg (3), Premier (3), Myers
   (3+Provident), Russon (3), the Dignity trio, and eight 2-location brands.
2. **One building, three licensees:** 1001 11th Ave SLC = City View + Eternity
   + Inspired. Just Cremations operates from the old Walker Provo building;
   Leavitt's Ogden also trades as "Cremation Society of Utah."
3. **Rebrands in flight:** Affordable F&C → Vista; Holbrook → Memorial
   Millcreek; Russon SLC closed. FCA suggests Serenity (Bluffdale) and the
   Vista/Affordable operation may be related — verify before treating as
   independent data points.
4. **Segment labels matter for averages:** cremation-focused/online-first
   (After, Utah Simple, Just Cremations, Kramer, Premier) vs full-service;
   IPS Crematory and Care Center of Utah are trade-only (exclude); SLCC
   mortuary school appears in directories (exclude).
5. **Directory noise:** iMortuary/Ever Loved lists include unlicensed
   cemeteries and stale entities. Vet everything against the DOPL pull.
   funeralOne-built sites (lindquist, leavitts, aarons, broomhead)
   intermittently 403 automated fetchers — collect in a real browser.
6. **No Utah law requires online GPL posting** — only the FTC Funeral Rule
   (GPL on request, in person). Utah's honest posting rate (~17 of ~44 brands)
   is actually above the 18% national rate, driven by the discount segment.

## Metro 2: Sacramento (decided; San Diego = metro 3)

**Correction to our own cite:** the CA law is **SB 658 (2011), operative
Jan 1, 2013, B&P Code §7685(b)** (not a 2021 bill). Default duty: a funeral
establishment with a website must post the 16 Funeral-Rule service categories
plus "GPL available upon request"; posting the actual GPL is the safe-harbor
alternative (§7685(b)(3)). So CA *nudges* — but the nudge worked:

- CFA/FCA 2019 census: 44–45% of CA homes posted full GPLs; **Sacramento 81%
  full-GPL-online (best of six areas; San Diego 54%)**.
- CFA 2022 (35 state capitals): national posting rate 18%; **Sacramento 70% —
  the best city in the study**.
- 2023: SCI/Dignity flipped — ~75% of Dignity sites now post GPLs.
- **Aug 2026 spot-check: Sacramento 10/10, San Diego 8/10** (7/9 among
  still-operating homes).

**The Dignity accelerant (works in every metro incl. SLC):** every Dignity
location serves a real GPL PDF at
`dignitymemorial.com/funeral-homes/{state}/{city}/{slug}/{id}/funeral-price-list`
(verified 5/5). Note: Crystal-Reports PDFs whose text layer defeats some
extractors — pypdf extracts cleanly; build against that. One PDF can cover
multiple FD licenses.

**Platform CDN patterns (cover most of the CA market):** funeralOne
(`cdn.f1connect.net/cdn/{id}/gpl/*.pdf`, `{domain}/cdn/{id}/layout/pdf/gpl.pdf`),
Duda (`irp.cdn-website.com/{id}/files/uploaded/*.pdf`), CFS
(`{domain}/general-price-list`). Google indexes GPL PDF text: dork
`"general price list" {city}`.

**CA gotchas:** the "available upon request" loophole cohort (~25–30% in
2019/2022) mostly ignores emailed requests (2/33 responded in FCA's test) —
budget a zero-yield residue; closed homes' GPLs linger online (Anderson-
Ragsdale, closed 11/2025) — cross-check license status at search.dca.ca.gov
before publishing; cemetery+mortuary combos mix interment pricing into the
GPL — parse those line items separately (outside Funeral Rule scope). CFA's
2019 report and 2022 appendix are free pre-built seed rosters.

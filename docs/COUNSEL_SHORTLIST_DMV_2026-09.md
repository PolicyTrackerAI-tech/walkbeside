## The shape of the engagement

The DMV needs **three state answers plus the federal architecture**. The
federal work (AKS/CMP, HIPAA business-associate status, the services
agreement) is the same package as before. DC is the country's densest
market for exactly that work, so the lead firm can be a DC
healthcare-regulatory shop that also covers the DC clearance.
Virginia's and Maryland's funeral-licensing questions are narrower. They are
best bought from lawyers who appear before those boards.

**Recommended structure:** one **lead firm** (federal + DC + services
agreement, coordinating the rest) plus **Virginia licensing counsel**
(the preneed question is the single most important state answer) plus
**Maryland licensing counsel** (lighter). Ask the lead firm whether it
has, or can refer, the two licensing lawyers. Firms routinely do.

**Budget framing (planning assumption, not a quote):** the Utah package
was scoped at $3,000–7,500. Three jurisdictions will cost more. Ask the lead
for a fixed fee on the federal + DC package, and ask the licensing lawyers
for flat-fee memos answering only the numbered questions in the clearance
drafts. The drafts exist precisely to make that cheap.

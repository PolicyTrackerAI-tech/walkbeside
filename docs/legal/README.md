# The legal drafting package (counsel-review-ready)

_Created 2026-08-18. Strategy: every deliverable in the counsel setup package
is pre-drafted here, so the engagement shrinks from "draft" to "confirm and
redline," which is faster and materially cheaper. The drafts encode the
2026-08-16 payer-viability research (~150 sources) and the positions already
enforced in the product's code._

## The rules that govern this folder

1. **Nothing here is legal advice, and nothing here gets executed before
   counsel sign-off.** Every file is a DRAFT prepared by the founder with AI
   assistance. The documented gate stands: counsel review blocks the first
   hospice signature, not the first conversation.
2. Sensitive judgment calls are flagged inline with **[COUNSEL]**. A firm's
   redline of these flags is the product we are buying.
3. When counsel finalizes a document, the draft here is replaced by the
   final and the change is noted in the file header.

## What we can apply before counsel (and what we cannot)

**Safe to apply now:** everything conversational and preparatory. Discovery
outreach and calls, the compliance packet, pilot-design conversations,
demoing, GPL collection, forming the Delaware entity mechanics that counsel
will paper anyway, and internal operating discipline that matches these
drafts.

**Not before counsel:** signing the pilot agreement or the services agreement
with any hospice; sending either document to a hospice as "our contract"
(sharing a draft marked draft during late-stage pilot talks is a judgment
call; default no); executing the LLC-to-C-corp conversion (tax consequences
need a professional read); making public legal claims ("HIPAA does not apply
to us") in marketing.

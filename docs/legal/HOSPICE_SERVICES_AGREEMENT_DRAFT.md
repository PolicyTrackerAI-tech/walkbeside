**BEREAVEMENT SUPPORT SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made as of [DATE] between **[Honest Funeral Co. entity —

("Honest Funeral") and **[HOSPICE LEGAL NAME]**, a Medicare-certified hospice
(CCN [___]) ("Hospice").

## Recitals

A. Medicare's hospice Conditions of Participation require Hospice to make an
organized program of bereavement services available to each patient's family
for up to one year following the patient's death (42 C.F.R. §418.64(d)), a
service Medicare does not separately reimburse.

B. Honest Funeral operates a free-to-family, neutral funeral-price education
and navigation service. Honest Funeral accepts no compensation of any kind
from funeral providers or insurers, accepts no compensation contingent on any
family's selection of any funeral provider, and presents funeral options
neutrally without recommending any particular provider.

C. Hospice wishes to make Honest Funeral's family-facing service available to
the families it serves as part of Hospice's bereavement and psychosocial
support program, and to procure the program services described below. The
parties intend this Agreement as procurement of bereavement support services
and not as payment for referrals in either direction, and they intend that no
protected health information be exchanged in either direction.

## 1. Services

1.1 **Family access.** Honest Funeral will make its family service available
at no charge to families of Hospice's admitted patients who choose to
activate it. Activation is exclusively self-service: Hospice may provide
families with activation materials (printed cards, a QR code, a link, or a
claim code) **only after admission**, and each family activates directly with
Honest Funeral and provides its own information. Honest Funeral's duties to
each activated family run to that family as a consumer service the family
voluntarily elects; nothing in this Agreement makes Honest Funeral the
provider of Hospice's clinical or counseling services. **[COUNSEL: this
sentence carries the dual-frame; confirm wording.]**

1.2 **Program services to Hospice.** Honest Funeral will provide Hospice:
(a) activation materials and reasonable quantities of program collateral;
(b) one orientation session for Hospice staff on how the program works and
what may and may not be said to families about it; (c) a quarterly program
summary containing only de-identified, aggregate information (Section 4)
suitable for inclusion in Hospice's bereavement program documentation; and
(d) access to a partner dashboard displaying the same aggregate information.

1.3 **Service description.** The family service consists of funeral-price
education, price comparison drawn from published and collected price lists,
assistance invoking the family's rights under the FTC Funeral Rule to
itemized pricing, neutral side-by-side presentation of options, and
documentation support. Honest Funeral does not arrange funerals, does not
select providers, does not handle family funds, does not sell merchandise,
and does not take custody of remains. Each family makes every arrangement
decision and executes every funeral contract directly with the provider of
its choice.

## 2. Honest Funeral's standing covenants

Honest Funeral covenants that, during the term and with respect to every
family served under this Agreement, it will not:

(a) contact any patient or family who has not first activated the service;
(b) recommend, rank, or prefer any specific funeral establishment; all vetted
options are presented in a neutral, disclosed order;
(c) accept any payment, referral fee, listing fee, or other consideration
from any funeral provider or insurer;
(d) charge any family any amount for anything;
(e) transmit or request patient or family identifying information to or from
Hospice; or
(f) permit Hospice's participation or payment under this Agreement to affect
the presentation, ranking, or description of Hospice or any other hospice in
any consumer-facing directory or tool operated by Honest Funeral.

## 3. Hospice's covenants

Hospice covenants that it will:

(a) distribute activation materials only to families of admitted patients or
families otherwise already in Hospice's care, and only after admission;
(b) not use the program, or the fact of it, in any pre-admission marketing,
advertising, or communication intended to induce selection of Hospice, and
not represent the program as conditioned on choosing or remaining with
Hospice; **[COUNSEL: beneficiary-inducement core; confirm scope.]**
(c) not transmit patient or family identifying information to Honest Funeral,
including census lists, referral lists, or contact information; and
(d) present the program to families accurately as an optional, free,
independent service.

## 4. No PHI; no business associate relationship

4.1 The parties intend and agree that no protected health information, as
defined at 45 C.F.R. §160.103, will be created, received, maintained, or
transmitted by Honest Funeral for or on behalf of Hospice under this
Agreement, and that Honest Funeral is not a business associate of Hospice.

4.2 All reporting to Hospice under this Agreement will consist solely of
aggregate information de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. §164.514,
with minimum cell sizes sufficient to prevent re-identification. The partner
dashboard will never display information about an identifiable family.
**[COUNSEL: confirm the de-identification standard reference and whether a

4.3 If the parties later agree to any service that would require Honest
Funeral to create or receive PHI for or on behalf of Hospice, the parties
will execute a business associate agreement before any such information is
exchanged, and until then neither party will exchange any.

## 9. Term and termination

9.1 Initial term of twelve months from the effective date, renewing
automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives
notice of non-renewal at least thirty days before renewal.

9.2 During the first term only, Hospice may terminate for convenience on
sixty days' written notice.

9.3 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice under Section
6.2, or for the other's uncured material breach after thirty days' notice.

9.4 Termination does not interrupt service to any family with an active
matter; Honest Funeral will continue serving activated families at no charge
notwithstanding termination. Sections 2(c), 2(f), 4, 8.2, and 10 survive.

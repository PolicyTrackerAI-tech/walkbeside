# Pilot success criteria

*Annex to the free 60-day pilot agreement between Honest Funeral and [Hospice Name]. Complete every blank together before signing, on the call. Agreeing the numbers now is what makes the end-of-pilot review a reading of results rather than a negotiation. The executive director or administrator sets each target; Honest Funeral records outcomes per case and reports against them.*

## The five measures

| # | Measure | How it is measured | Target for this pilot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Families served** | Distinct families who enrolled themselves through [Hospice Name]'s link or card and whose case was opened during the pilot window. | [10 to 15] families |
| 2 | **Family satisfaction** | A direct ask to each family at case close, scored 1 to 5, averaged across all closed cases. | Average of at least [4.0] out of 5 |
| 3 | **Documented savings** | Each family's original itemized quote minus what the family actually paid, recorded per case and counted only where a documented original quote exists. | Average of at least $[_____] per family |
| 4 | **Staff hours returned** | [Hospice Name]'s own estimate of bereavement-team time not spent on funeral-pricing and after-death paperwork questions, made together at the week-6 review. | At least [_____] hours over the pilot |
| 5 | **Time to resolution** | Days from case opened to outcome recorded, averaged across closed cases. | Average of no more than [_____] days |

## How the numbers stay honest

- Every figure is reported with its n. If [8] families enroll, the report says [8].
- Savings are documented, never estimated. A case without an original quote contributes no savings figure.
- Satisfaction is asked by us, not by hospice staff, and reported as given.
- [Hospice Name] receives aggregate results only, never an individual family's case details.

## What the numbers decide

If the targets are met, the conversation turns to whether to continue at a paid annual subscription, sized by census. If they are not met, the pilot ends, [Hospice Name] owes nothing, and keeps the final report either way.

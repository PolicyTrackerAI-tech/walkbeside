# The compliance and pilot packet

The sendable set that answers a hospice's diligence before it is asked for. The standing rule from the execution plan (workstream A, `docs/EXECUTION_PLAN_2026-08.md`): **the compliance packet ships with every pilot conversation, unprompted.** The channel is scarred by funeral-sector tools that used hospices as lead generators; the objection gets answered by architecture, in writing, not by reassurance on a call.

## What's in it

| File | What it is | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| [`AKS_ONE_PAGER.md`](AKS_ONE_PAGER.md) | The anti-kickback and referral-law position on one page: post-admission delivery, flat fee with no volume linkage, 418.64(d) procurement framing, family-initiated activation, no PHI transmitted, no supply-side money ever. | Compliance officer, ED |
| [`SECURITY_TRUST_PACKET.md`](SECURITY_TRUST_PACKET.md) | The security and privacy summary the discovery script promises (section 6.3): what we store, who can see it, what the hospice transmits (nothing), subprocessors, and what we do not have yet. | Compliance, IT |
| [`PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md`](PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md) | The written-up-front success criteria annex: five measures with blanks the ED fills in on the call. | ED |

Two companion documents live one level up and complete the set: [`../PILOT_AGREEMENT.md`](../PILOT_AGREEMENT.md) (what gets signed) and [`../ROI_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md`](../ROI_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md) (the proof sheet the pilot ends on).

## What gets sent when

Timing follows [`../OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md`](../OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md) and [`../DISCOVERY_SCRIPT.md`](../DISCOVERY_SCRIPT.md).

| Moment | Attach |
|---|---|
| Cold outreach (Email 1, Email 2) | Nothing from this folder. Email 2 offers the one-page outline ([`../HOSPICE_ONEPAGER.md`](../HOSPICE_ONEPAGER.md)) only. Cold emails stay clean. |
| Discovery follow-up, same day as the call | [`AKS_ONE_PAGER.md`](AKS_ONE_PAGER.md) and [`SECURITY_TRUST_PACKET.md`](SECURITY_TRUST_PACKET.md), alongside the one-pager. Sent unprompted with the thank-you note, whether or not compliance came up on the call. If the call surfaced a BAA or paperwork request (discovery script 6.3), name the security packet explicitly in the note. |
| Pilot proposal / pitch call | [`../PILOT_AGREEMENT.md`](../PILOT_AGREEMENT.md) draft plus [`PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md`](PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md) with targets blank, filled in together on the call. Re-attach the two compliance documents whenever a new stakeholder (ED, compliance officer) joins the thread. |
| Pilot signature | The completed [`PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md`](PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md), no blanks, annexed to the signed agreement. |
| Week-9 results review | The filled [`../ROI_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md`](../ROI_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md) proof sheet. |

## Rules for every send

- Fill every [bracketed field] and export to PDF before sending. Nothing goes out with a blank.
- The founder approves the packet and each material change to it (execution plan: [C] assembles, [F] approves).
- Positions marked "pending counsel review" keep that marking until the counsel memo lands. If a hospice asks about them, the answer is exactly that: our research position, counsel review in progress.
- Never claim a control or certification that does not exist. If the security packet says we lack something, that line stays until the thing ships. Honesty is the brand; it is also what a compliance officer is testing for.
- These files are the masters. Edit them here by PR so the sent artifact always matches the repo.

## What the program is

Honest Funeral gives grieving families free, neutral help with funeral pricing and after-death paperwork. Families pay nothing. Funeral homes and insurers pay nothing and are paid nothing. The hospice pays a flat annual subscription, sized by census, as procurement of bereavement support it is already required to provide.

## The position, in one paragraph

Funeral services are not federally payable, so a funeral referral itself sits outside the Anti-Kickback Statute and the civil monetary penalty rules on beneficiary inducement. The recognized danger vector for a benefit like this one is different: a free family benefit used to induce the selection of a hospice. The program is built against that vector. The benefit is delivered after admission only and never appears in pre-admission marketing. The hospice's payment is procurement of bereavement and psychosocial support, an obligation the hospice already carries under 42 CFR 418.64(d), and is not payment for referrals in either direction. OIG guidance treats benefits disclosed to a patient only after provider selection as outside the inducement bar; our research reads OIG Advisory Opinion 00-3 as the closest authority (pending counsel review). On the supply side, no money moves between Honest Funeral and any funeral home, which also keeps every home presented to a family clean under New York Public Health Law §3450 and Virginia's anti-cappers statute.

## The six design rules

| Rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Post-admission delivery only** | The resource reaches a family only after hospice admission. It never appears in pre-admission marketing and may not be used to encourage selection of the hospice. This is a written term of the pilot agreement. |
| **Flat fee, no volume linkage** | Pricing is a census-tiered flat annual subscription. Nothing in the price varies with referrals, activations, or families served. Per-family pricing was considered and rejected because it patterns like a referral fee. Invoices read "bereavement support program" with the tier name. |
| **Bereavement-support procurement** | The contract buys help delivering the hospice's own 42 CFR 418.64(d) bereavement obligation, roughly thirteen months of post-death support that Medicare requires but does not separately reimburse. It does not buy marketing, referrals, or access to families. |
| **Family-initiated activation** | The hospice hands the family a link or card. The family decides whether to enroll, directly with us. The platform never initiates contact with a bereaved family or next of kin, which also keeps the program clear of state restrictions on at-need solicitation. |
| **No PHI transmitted** | The hospice sends us nothing about any patient or family. Because families enroll themselves, no protected health information flows from the hospice to us, and no business associate agreement is required by construction (pending counsel review). Reporting back to the hospice is aggregate and de-identified, and small cohorts are suppressed rather than shown. |
| **No supply-side revenue, ever** | We take no money from funeral homes or insurers, pay nothing for placement, and sell no placement. Families see the vetted funeral homes near them, ordered by proximity alone, and choose for themselves. Payment never changes how any provider is presented on our site. |

## If you have seen this go wrong before

Some funeral-sector startups have used hospices as lead generators, taking decedent or family data from the provider and contacting families directly. This program is designed to be the opposite of that pattern. No data flows from the hospice. No family is ever contacted uninvited. No funeral provider pays us anything. We would rather show you the architecture than ask you for trust.

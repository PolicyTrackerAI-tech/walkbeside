# Utah hospice target list — discovery wave 1

_Researched 2026-08-18 from CMS Care Compare, the FY2024 Medicare hospice
utilization file, CAHPS star data, and public team pages. Companion to
`OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md` (templates) and `DISCOVERY_SCRIPT.md` (the call).
Contact names come from public pages and can go stale; verify title on the
call. ADC = FY2024 Medicare ADC (true census runs ~10–15% higher)._

**Universe:** 84 Medicare-certified Utah hospices (Aug 2026 roster). ~28 CCNs
(~25 organizations) fit the ICP (ADC 20–150, independent/regional, no flags).
The roster is frozen: CMS imposed a nationwide temporary moratorium on new
hospice Medicare enrollments effective May 13, 2026 — every name below is an
incumbent feeling program-integrity heat, which is exactly the climate where a
conflict-free bereavement-support benefit reads as a "we're the good guys"
purchase.

**Pitch-angle notes:** the three 2★-CAHPS Tier-1 names (Rocky Mountain,
Atlas, Utah Home Health & Hospice) have the strongest family-experience
improvement angle (CAHPS includes emotional/spiritual-support measures). CNS
is a mission-aligned nonprofit: lead with mission + staff-hours, and keep the
sponsorship tier in reserve on price. Bereavement coordinators are almost
never published: on the main line, ask for "the person who runs your
13-month bereavement program."

## Watch-outs (never pitch / verify first)

1. **Never pitch: Summit Hospice** — $1,045,944 False Claims Act settlement
   (DOJ D-Utah, March 2023; claims billed without documented terminal
   illness). The surviving St. George entity keeps the brand. Skip entirely.
2. **Gone:** Wasatch Hospice, Magna — Medicare agreement terminated June 11,
   2025. Tender Care WVC was absorbed by Mission Healthcare.
3. **No-track-record entrants (deprioritize, don't accuse):** Serenity
   Hospice of Utah (PG, 2023, unusually fast ramp — looks like a real local
   startup but verify), Elite (Lehi 2024), BrightenLife (St George 2024),
   Sequoia (Midvale 2025), Desert Haven (Washington 2025).
4. **Counsel conflicts:** Bristol Hospice (SLC HQ) is on the conflicts list
   in `docs/COUNSEL_SHORTLIST_2026-08.md`.

## Re-pull instructions (keep the list fresh)

- Roster/ownership/phones: CMS Care Compare "Hospice – General Information"
  dataset `yc9t-dgbk`, filter state=UT (84 rows).
- **Size:** "Medicare Post-Acute Care Utilization – Hospice by Geography and
  Provider" FY2024, dataset `4e73f1b5-82cb-4682-8ad2-28493f0b6840`, filter
  STATE=UT, SMRY_CTGRY=PROVIDER. **ADC = TOT_SRVC_DAYS ÷ 366**; ~$65–70k
  Medicare revenue per ADC-year.
- CAHPS: dataset `gxki-hrr8`, measure SUMMARY_STAR_RATING (a star existing at
  all means ≥75 completed surveys — itself a size signal).
- Ownership chains: CMS "Hospice All Owners" file. State licensing: Utah DHHS
  DLBC facility finder + Utah SGID health-facilities download.
